I spent three years helping students turn rough ideas into working circuits. Now I'm learning to manage that journey at scale.

My background is hands-on electronics: a B.Sc. in Instrumentation and an M.Sc. in Electronics from Sardar Patel University, followed by close to three years as a Senior STEM & Robotics Coach at STEMBOTIX in Gujarat. I taught Arduino, IoT, drones and PCB design, and guided student teams through national innovation programs.

In 2026 I completed Coursera certificates in PCB design and Google AI, and started the Master of Engineering Management at the University of Windsor. I want to combine technical depth with product, project and innovation management for engineering teams in Canada.
